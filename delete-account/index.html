Delete your Glowlarp account and data

Last updated: 25 October 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Glowlarp and sign in.
  2. Tap the gear icon, then Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted right away.

What we delete

  • Your Glowlarp account and sign-in (your Google account itself is not affected)
  • Your name and the onboarding answers you gave
  • Your photos and the stored image files
  • Your face analyses and reports
  • Your try-on images (your looks) and their files
  • Your AI Stylist chats
  • Your Looks balance and Looks history
  • Any guest data from your phone that was linked to your account

What we keep

  • Google Play and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their own policies. We can't delete those for you, and they contain no photos.
  • Backups and server logs are deleted on their normal schedule.

Smaller deletions without closing your account

  • Delete one look: open it in your Library and tap Delete.
  • Clear your Stylist chat: tap the trash icon in the Stylist tab.

If you can't open the app

Email glowlarp.app@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Google account, with the subject "Delete my Glowlarp account". We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

If you never signed in

Guest data is deleted automatically 30 days after you first used the app, no matter how recently you used it. If you still hold Looks you bought as a guest, we keep your data so you don't lose them: sign in to move your Looks to an account. Once you have used all your Looks, your guest data is deleted the next time our daily cleanup runs.

More details are in our Privacy Policy.
