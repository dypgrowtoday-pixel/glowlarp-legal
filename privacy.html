Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Glowlarp uses your selfie to suggest haircuts and show them on your own photo. We collect only what the app needs to do that, we never sell your data, and you can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Glowlarp is run by Shantanu Mishra, an individual developer based in India ("we", "us"). Contact: glowlarp.app@gmail.com.

Your face and photos

When you add a selfie, our AI estimates your face shape and facial features to suggest haircuts and create try-on images. We use this only to provide those features. We do not use it to identify you, recognise you in other photos, or build a face-recognition profile. We ask for your explicit consent before your first photo is processed. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your data in Settings.

Only upload photos of yourself. Do not upload photos of other people or of anyone under 18.

How long we keep it

  • Photos, analyses, try-on images and Stylist chats: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • If you use Glowlarp without an account: your guest data (photos, analyses, try-on images and chats) is deleted automatically 30 days after you first used the app. If you hold Looks you bought as a guest, we keep your data until you sign in to an account or use up your Looks.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to restore your Looks and as tax and accounting law requires.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your photos and their files, analyses, try-on images, chats, Looks history and account from our systems, and your sign-in account at Clerk. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their policies.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and all your data: Settings → Delete Account.
  • Delete a single look: open it in your Library and delete it.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion: email glowlarp.app@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.
  • If you live in the EU, UK or another place with data protection laws, you may also have the right to object, to restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority.
  • India: our grievance contact is Shantanu Mishra, glowlarp.app@gmail.com.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal data.
  • We don't show ads and we don't share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't use your photos to market Glowlarp.

Age limit

Glowlarp is for adults 18 and older only. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a minor has used Glowlarp, email us and we will delete their data.

Security

We use encrypted connections, private storage for your photos and limited access to the data. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise that a breach will never happen. If one affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Changes

We will update this policy when the app changes. If a change is important, we will tell you in the app before it applies.
